Build a new analytics module called “INS (Inflection Signal Score)” that runs alongside my existing VQS, GVS, and COS system.

The goal is to detect early-stage breakout stocks BEFORE they show up in high COS scores.

This system should NOT replace COS — it should complement it as a leading indicator.

---

## 1. DATA INPUTS

For each stock ticker, pull or accept these inputs:

- Price history (daily close, volume)
- Revenue growth (YoY, QoQ if available)
- Earnings surprise history (actual vs estimated)
- Analyst revisions trend (upgrades/downgrades if available)
- Sector performance index (relative strength vs S&P)
- Volatility (20D and 60D)
- Market cap

If full fundamentals are unavailable, allow fallback to price/volume-only mode.

---

## 2. FEATURE ENGINEERING

Compute the following features:

### A. Delta GVS (Momentum Acceleration)
Measure rate of change in momentum:

- GVS_current minus GVS_14d_ago
- GVS_14d_ago minus GVS_30d_ago

Normalize to 0–100 scale.

---

### B. Delta VQS (Fundamental Re-rating)
Measure improvement in fundamentals:

- revenue growth acceleration
- margin expansion trend
- earnings revision direction (positive/negative slope)

Output: 0–100 score

---

### C. Volume Acceleration Score
Detect institutional accumulation:

- 20-day average volume vs 5-day average volume
- ratio of up-volume vs down-volume
- breakout volume spikes (>2x avg)

Normalize into 0–100 scale

---

### D. Earnings Surprise Slope
Compute:

- (recent EPS surprise % – prior EPS surprise %)
- trend of beats over last 3–4 quarters

Output: 0–100 score

---

### E. Narrative Momentum Proxy
If NLP is not available, approximate using:

- sector-relative performance strength
- 20D relative strength vs SPY
- number of consecutive green days
- gap-up frequency in last 30 days

Output: 0–100 score

---

## 3. INS FORMULA (CORE ENGINE)

Compute final INS score:

INS =

0.25 * Delta_GVS +
0.20 * Volume_Acceleration +
0.20 * Narrative_Momentum +
0.20 * Earnings_Surprise_Slope +
0.15 * Delta_VQS

Normalize final output to 0–100.

---

## 4. SIGNAL CLASSIFICATION

Return classification labels:

- 80–100 = “EXPLOSIVE EARLY BREAKOUT (NBIS-STAGE)”
- 65–79 = “EARLY MOMENTUM BUILDING”
- 50–64 = “NEUTRAL / DEVELOPING”
- 30–49 = “WEAK / NO EDGE”
- <30 = “AVOID”

---

## 5. RELATIONSHIP TO COS SYSTEM

Also display:

COS vs INS divergence:

- If INS high (>70) AND COS low (<60):
  → “EARLY OPPORTUNITY (pre-COS breakout candidate)”

- If COS high AND INS declining:
  → “LATE STAGE / MOMENTUM PEAK RISK”

- If both high:
  → “HIGH CONVICTION TREND (CRDO-TYPE STOCK)”

---

## 6. OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS

For each stock display:

- VQS
- GVS
- COS
- INS (NEW)
- classification label
- divergence tag (if applicable)

---

## 7. UI REQUIREMENTS

Build a simple dashboard:

- sortable table of tickers
- color coding:
  - INS (green = high)
  - COS (blue = strong)
  - divergence warning (yellow/red)

- ability to filter:
  - INS > 70
  - COS > 70
  - INS rising / falling

---

## 8. GOAL OF SYSTEM

This system should identify stocks that:
- are NOT yet obvious winners in COS
- but are showing early institutional accumulation and narrative shift

These are the “NBIS / early CRDO stage” setups BEFORE they become widely recognized.

---

## 9. IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE

INS is intentionally a LEADING indicator.
COS is a CONFIRMATION indicator.

INS should lead COS by 2–6 weeks in emerging winners.
