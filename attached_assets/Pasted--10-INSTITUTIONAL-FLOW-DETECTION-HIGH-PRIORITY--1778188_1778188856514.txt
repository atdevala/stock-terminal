10. INSTITUTIONAL FLOW DETECTION (HIGH PRIORITY)
--------------------------------------------------

Create a new feature called:

“Accumulation Confidence Score (ACS)”

Purpose:
Detect whether institutions are quietly accumulating shares.

Use:
- up-volume vs down-volume
- consecutive accumulation days
- closing near highs of day
- low-volatility price compression before breakout
- abnormal volume spikes
- relative strength vs SPY

Formula:

ACS =

0.30 * UpVolumeStrength +
0.25 * RelativeStrength +
0.20 * PriceCompression +
0.15 * BreakoutVolume +
0.10 * ClosingStrength

Normalize:
0–100

Interpretation:
- 80+ = strong institutional accumulation
- 60–79 = moderate accumulation
- <60 = weak/no accumulation

Display ACS on all tables.

11. FALSE BREAKOUT FILTER (VERY IMPORTANT)
--------------------------------------------------

Create a filter that REDUCES scores when:

- INS spikes too quickly without COS confirmation
- volume spikes only 1 day then collapses
- stock gaps up but fades intraday repeatedly
- volatility becomes extreme without follow-through
- price rises while relative strength weakens

Create:

“False Breakout Risk Score (FBRS)”

0–100 scale:
- High score = dangerous setup
- Low score = cleaner breakout structure

If FBRS > 70:
Display:
“CAUTION: POSSIBLE HYPE-DRIVEN MOVE”
